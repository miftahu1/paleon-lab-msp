Our Story

Why we started Bloom & Co.

In 2019, we noticed something: incredible makers were creating beautiful things, but had no way to reach customers beyond local markets. Meanwhile, people were craving authenticity—real things made by real hands.

Bloom & Co was born from that gap. We curate, we tell stories, and we handle the logistics so makers can focus on making.

Today, we work with over 80 independent artisans across 23 states. Every item in our collection is made in batches of 50 or fewer.
